Taste of Redemption

A narrative-driven VR experience exploring war, survival, and human connection through cooking.

Overview

Players navigate environments, collect ingredients, and engage with a story about the human consequences of war. The audio direction prioritizes realistic environmental sound, positional cues for spatial awareness, and music that supports narrative atmosphere.
