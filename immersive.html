Immersive by Your Own Music

An interactive sound installation that transforms the human body’s natural rhythms into a personal musical experience.

Overview

By capturing heartbeat and breathing sounds in real time, the system translates physiological rhythms into musical parameters. A three-stage adaptive music structure gradually introduces richer layers, textures, and harmonies in response to the participant’s behavior.

My Contributions

  • Designed a responsive generative music system driven by heartbeat and breathing data.
  • Developed real-time heartbeat BPM analysis using Python.
  • Designed adaptive transitions, randomized timbres, and layered musical feedback.
  • Integrated Pure Data, Max, and DAW-based production into an audiovisual experience.
