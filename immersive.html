The project

A quiet conversation with your own rhythm.

What changes when we begin to listen to ourselves? This installation turns the usually unnoticed sounds of heartbeat and breathing into the basis of a personal musical experience. Live analysis connects the participant’s rhythms to a composition that gradually opens up in texture, register, and detail.

01

Make the connection audible.

The journey begins with the unprocessed sounds of breathing and heartbeat, so participants can recognize their own input. As the measured rhythms become more coordinated, the balance shifts toward musical material. This change can reverse, keeping the response connected to the participant.

02

Build a musical sense of progression.

Three stages shape the experience. The first introduces the relationship between body and music. The second adds low-register instruments and electric piano, with beat-triggered timbre variation. The final stage opens into higher-register and reversed sounds, offering a distinct change in depth and color.

03

Let the system support the listening.

Python estimates heartbeat BPM and beat triggers; Pure Data and the DAW support the musical system. BPM data also feeds Max for visual feedback. Proximity sensing and spoken guidance help participants enter the experience, while the changing sound remains the central point of attention.
