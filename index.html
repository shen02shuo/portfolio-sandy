About

Shuo Shen

I’m Shuo Shen, a sound designer working across creative and technical audio, with a background at the Beijing Film Academy’s School of Sound.

My experience includes sound design and implementation for the shipped AAA title NARAKA: BLADEPOINT and audio work on Persona 5: The Phantom X, alongside commercial films, branded content and widely viewed short-form series.

I combine a critical ear with practical implementation and audio quality evaluation, including AI-generated sound and speech. I work in English and Chinese on productions for domestic and international audiences.
