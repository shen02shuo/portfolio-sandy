Ground

An experimental musical interface that turns physical movement into an interactive performance.

Overview

Inspired by the Chinese Five Elements and traditional pentatonic music, Ground assigns musical identities to spatial zones. Moving between zones changes the composition; overlapping regions introduce combinations. Primary and auxiliary audio states help preserve musical continuity.
